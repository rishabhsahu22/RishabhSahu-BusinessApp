About SetuChain Advisory

Setu means “bridge”. We bridge the gap between how a supply chain is planned on paper and how it actually runs on the shop floor, in the warehouse and on the road.

What we do

Diagnose supply chain problems, design better networks and inventory policies, and stay on the ground until the savings show up in the P&L.

Target customers

Mid-size manufacturers, MSME clusters, FMCG & pharma distributors, and 3PLs with annual revenue of ₹50–1,000 Cr.

Service area

Gujarat, Maharashtra, Rajasthan and the NCR — the Western and Eastern Dedicated Freight Corridor belts — with remote diagnostics pan-India.

Value proposition

Measurable results: typically 8–15% lower logistics cost and 20–30% less inventory, with full project transparency through our online tracker.

The problem our app solves

Clients of consulting firms rarely know where their project stands or what savings have been delivered until the next review meeting, and managers lack one view of which engagements are on track. Our app gives clients a self-service Project Tracker and gives our managers a live Engagement Dashboard.
